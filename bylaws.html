Bylaws of the Modern Nonprofit Student Organization

As Recognized by the Board of Directors

Article III – Board of Directors

Section 1: Composition

  • One (1) Chairman (commonly referred to as “The Chair”)
  • One (1) Vice Chairman (“Vice Chair”)
  • Three (3) Directors

Section 2: Authority and Duties

  • The Chairman shall serve as the principal executive officer and preside over all meetings.
  • The Vice Chair shall support the Chair and assume leadership duties in the Chair’s absence.
  • The Directors shall participate equally in governance decisions and policy votes.

Section 3: Decision-Making and Overrides

  • All Board decisions require a simple majority to pass.
  • The Chairman may exercise executive authority on urgent matters or to break ties.
  • Override Clause: If the Vice Chair and all three Directors jointly oppose a decision made by the Chair, that decision shall be overridden and nullified.

Article IV – Terms of Office

Section 1: Board of Directors

  • Chairman: Serves a term of three (3) years. May be re-elected.
  • Vice Chairman: Serves a term of three (3) years. May be re-elected.
  • Directors: Each serves a term of five (5) years. May be re-elected.

Section 2: Youth Delegates

  • Youth Delegates serve a term of one (1) year.
  • Terms may be renewed annually with no limit, unless otherwise restricted by age eligibility.
  • Age Limit: Delegates may serve as youth representatives until the age of 23. Upon reaching the age of 23, they may no longer renew as a Youth Delegate but may apply to become a general Member or Staff of the MNSO.

Section 3: Members

  • General members of the MNSO serve a one (1) year term, renewable indefinitely.
  • Membership is open to individuals who align with the goals and mission of the organization, regardless of age.
Published and certified by the MNSO Board of Directors. Document Version: July 2025.
