A National Vision

The United States stands at a pivotal moment in history. Our institutions face new pressures, our children grow up in an increasingly complex digital world, and global competition intensifies across technology, economics, and diplomacy.

This platform proposes a forward-looking strategy focused on strengthening education, protecting youth, advancing technological leadership, and maintaining responsible governance.

Rebuilding American Education

The future of the nation depends on an educated population capable of critical reasoning and informed citizenship.

Key Commitments

  • Reform education curriculum to prioritize analytical thinking and problem solving.
  • Establish Societal Studies as a core academic discipline.
  • Expand instruction in economics, financial literacy, geography, and global systems.
  • Teach students to recognize misinformation, propaganda, and digital manipulation.
  • Focus education on teaching students how to think, not what to think.

Protecting Childhood in the Digital Age

Modern social media platforms increasingly expose children to harmful content, extremist ideology, and psychological manipulation during key developmental years.

Key Commitments

  • Prohibit social media access for individuals under the age of 15.
  • Require technology companies to implement effective age verification systems.
  • Encourage healthier youth development through education, sports, and community engagement.

American Leadership in Space and Artificial Intelligence

The next era of human progress will be defined by technological advancement. The United States must lead that future.

Aerospace Leadership

  • Expand national investment in aerospace research and space exploration.
  • Support programs enabling permanent human presence beyond Earth.
  • Strengthen partnerships with private space industries and research institutions.

Artificial Intelligence Governance

  • Accelerate American innovation in artificial intelligence.
  • Implement strong national regulatory oversight.
  • Ensure government accountability in high-risk AI development.

Opportunity Through Education and Healthcare

A prosperous nation requires both an educated population and a healthy workforce.

  • Provide tuition-free access to public universities for American citizens.
  • Invest in the development of engineers, scientists, teachers, and healthcare professionals.
  • Guarantee universal access to healthcare services.
  • Develop efficient systems modeled on successful international frameworks.

Responsible Government and Fiscal Discipline

Government must use public resources efficiently and responsibly.

  • Conduct audits of federal spending programs.
  • Eliminate redundant and ineffective programs.
  • Increase transparency in federal budgeting.
  • Prioritize investment in education, infrastructure, and technology.

Diplomacy and Global Stability

The United States must lead the world through strength, diplomacy, and responsible engagement.

  • Expand diplomatic dialogue with allies and competitors.
  • Reduce geopolitical tensions through negotiation.
  • Maintain military presence where necessary to deter conflict.
  • Strengthen cooperation through international institutions and international law.
