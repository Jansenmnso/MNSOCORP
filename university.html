Our Mission

MNSO exists to create academic bridges between institutions around the world. This portal welcomes members of L’université de MNSO to pursue their journey toward excellence at the American University of Paris. We value global citizenship, cultural exchange, and institutional prestige.

“The MNSO works by bridging gaps between cultures and universities across the globe.”
