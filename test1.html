About the MNSO

The Modern Nonpartisan Student Organization (MNSO) is a youth-led initiative focused on civic education, journalism, and political dialogue. Through publications, podcasts, and educational programs, the MNSO encourages students to engage thoughtfully with public policy, government, and the democratic process.
