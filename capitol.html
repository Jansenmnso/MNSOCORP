MNSO Day at the Texas State Capitol

July • Austin, Texas — A hands-on civic experience for the next generation of leaders.

Event Overview

This July, the Modern Nonprofit Student Organization (MNSO) will host a delegation of youth at the Texas State Capitol in Austin. This experience is designed to give students direct exposure to state government, civic institutions, and the democratic process.

Participants will engage with a wide range of political perspectives, learn the historical significance of the Capitol, and observe how laws and public policy are shaped in real time.

What to Expect

Capitol Tour

A guided tour of the Texas State Capitol, focusing on its history and role in governance.

Legislative Meetings

Opportunities to meet state legislators and staff from different political perspectives.

Live Hearings

Observation of House or Senate hearings and floor sessions when in session.

Civic Education

Discussions on civic responsibility, state government, and respectful political dialogue.

Why This Matters

MNSO believes that meaningful civic engagement begins with education and firsthand experience. This event encourages informed participation and long-term public service.
