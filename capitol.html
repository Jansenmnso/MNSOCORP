MNSO Day at the Texas State Capitol

July • Austin, Texas — A hands-on civic experience for the next generation of leaders.

Event Overview

This July, the Modern Nonprofit Student Organization (MNSO) will host a delegation of youth at the Texas State Capitol in Austin. This experience is designed to give students direct exposure to state government, civic institutions, and the democratic process.

Participants will engage with a wide range of political perspectives, learn the historical significance of the Capitol, and observe how laws and public policy are shaped in real time.

What to Expect

Capitol Tour

A guided tour of the Texas State Capitol, focusing on its history, architecture, and role in Texas governance.

Legislative Meetings

Opportunities to meet and speak with state legislators and staff from a variety of political backgrounds, when available.

Live Hearings

Observation of House or Senate hearings and floor sessions to see the legislative process in action.

Civic Education

Discussions on the importance of state government, civic engagement, and respectful political dialogue.

Why This Matters

MNSO believes that meaningful civic engagement begins with education and firsthand experience. By bringing students directly into the seat of state government, this event encourages informed participation, open dialogue, and long-term public service.
