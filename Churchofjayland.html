The Holy Lutheran Church of Jayland

The Church of Jayland was established in 2020 for the purpose of sharing God's faith in a protected environment for youth. The Church continues to operate, maintain historical records, and conduct ceremonial services. The Church is fully known as The Holy Lutheran Church of Jayland.
